FAQ

Before you operate

What files does CDG Doctor work with?

The application works with CD+G graphics files and karaoke ZIP packages. When matching audio is available, it can be paired for preview and synchronisation work.

Does it overwrite my original song?

The normal workflow uses a working/preview copy. Keep the original CDG, matching audio and original ZIP, and save finished work as a new/versioned output.

Can it magically recover any destroyed lyric?

No. CD+G stores graphics commands, not a hidden text transcript. Recovery depends on what useful graphics, timing and presentation information still survives. When words are genuinely destroyed, you may need to provide the correct lyric text.

Why might Smart Roll-up leave part of a song unchanged?

That is deliberate safety behaviour. If a section uses an unsupported presentation style or does not have enough safe CD+G packet budget, Doctor can preserve that source-authored section rather than forcing an unreadable conversion.

Should I repair an old “Doctor Edit” again?

For serious reconstruction or presentation conversion, start from the original unconverted CDG whenever possible. An earlier edited output may already have had source packets changed or neutralised.
